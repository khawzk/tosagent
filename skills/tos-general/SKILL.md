# 神魔之塔 General

Use this skill for questions about the Traditional Chinese version of Tower of Saviors (神魔之塔, TOS): game systems, cards, skills, enemy mechanics, teams, stages, events, resources, and player advice. Treat version- and region-dependent details as live research, not permanent facts.

## Research approach

For current events, card strength, stage solutions, resources, and account advice, browse before answering. Search the Bahamut TOS board (`bsn=23805`) in Traditional Chinese; use the board's latest posts, pinned guides, relevant tags, and the newest replies in an active thread. See [references/bahamut-sources.md](references/bahamut-sources.md) for live search entry points and current source checks.

Use this source order:

1. Current in-game notices and official Madhead/Tower of Saviors pages for exact rules, dates, rewards, and eligibility.
2. Bahamut posts marked `官方情報` when they reproduce the official notice; confirm important claims against the official notice when available.
3. Current, maintained Bahamut guides and stage-clear threads for player-tested interpretations and strategies.
4. Wikis, card databases, skill searchers, videos, and older forum posts for discovery or historical context; verify volatile facts elsewhere.

For a live question, search the exact event/stage/card name and the current month or version, then search a second way for mechanics or alternatives. Check the post's original date, last edit, stated game version, region, and latest relevant replies. A recent reply does not necessarily mean the guide itself was updated.

Apply a strict freshness gate to volatile facts: a community source must have been published or substantively updated within the last 90 days and match the current game version, region, and still-open event. Older community material is for discovery or historical questions only; never use it as evidence for today's card strength, event details, mechanics, team route, or resource advice. A maintained index can help locate a topic, but does not make each linked section current. Verify exact dates and rewards against an official notice that is still valid today. If no fresh, matching source is available, say what is unknown and ask for a current screenshot or details instead of falling back to stale advice. Remove expired examples from the source index.

If Bahamut blocks page retrieval or exposes only search previews, be explicit about that limitation. Do not imply that you read a full post when you only saw its title/snippet. Use another accessible source, ask the user to share the relevant text or screenshot, or give a clearly labeled provisional answer.

## Answering gameplay questions

- Match the player's actual goal: clear once, earn all achievements, farm quickly, use a specific leader, avoid spending stones, or build from their box. Ask only for missing details that can change the answer.
- For team building, inspect every floor and its mechanics first. Match each mechanic to a specific team answer, then give the lineup, required leader/friend arrangement, card evolutions or skill states, Dragoncraft/equipment, time card, and skill order only when those details are supported by the source. Separate mandatory pieces from substitutions and explain what a substitution loses.
- If the player shares a card box, distinguish cards they own from suggested cards they do not own. Do not infer ownership from a public guide, another player's box, or a screenshot that does not show the relevant card.
- Explain the game term in plain Chinese before using community shorthand. Preserve exact enemy-skill or card wording where a small wording difference changes the solution; explain the practical consequence in your own words.
- Distinguish a tested clear, a copied script/turn-by-turn route, a theoretical team, and personal opinion. Note randomness, damage checks, turn order, and equipment requirements that can make a route fail.
- Card rankings, pull advice, and resource priorities are subjective and change quickly. Tie recommendations to the player's collection, resource budget, play style, and the current event window. Do not tell the player to spend premium currency without making the cost and alternatives clear.
- For event calendars, state the server/region and timezone if known, list exact start/end times, and verify that the notice is still active. Ask which game region the player uses if eligibility can differ by region.

## Working with the player

Give the useful answer first, then concise reasoning and direct sources. For a team or mechanic explanation, a floor-by-floor table is often clearer than a long paragraph. Cite the specific official notice or Bahamut guide supporting time-sensitive claims and label community judgment as community judgment.

Never request or retain passwords, one-time codes, transfer codes, session tokens, or payment details. If a screenshot includes account identifiers or private data, avoid repeating them and ask for a redacted image when needed.

This skill is for game knowledge and advice. Do not use it to click through the game, claim rewards, automate battles, simulate taps, inspect game memory/traffic, or modify the client. The official service terms prohibit bots and unauthorized automated execution software; keep game actions with the player unless Madhead provides explicit permission for a specific integration.
